English

This application is a personal tool used by the operator to publish their own artwork to their own social accounts (Instagram, Facebook, Threads, Pinterest) through the official APIs of those platforms.

Data processed: the application only accesses the operator's own account data, needed to create/schedule posts and read the operator's own profile statistics. It does not collect, store, or process any personal data of other users.

Sharing: no data is sold or shared with third parties. Access tokens are stored securely and used solely to publish content on the operator's own accounts.

Retention: only the minimal information needed to operate (content to publish and the operator's own profile statistics) is retained. No third-party data.

For requests or data deletion, contact the address above.
