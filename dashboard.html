Da qui partono le navi verso l'ignoto e qui tornano i naviganti, guidati dalla fiamma in cima al faro nelle notti di tempesta. From here, ships set sail toward the unknown, and here sailors return, guided by the flame atop the lighthouse on stormy nights. There's something melancholic and poetic about a frontier town like this one. Would you sail off toward the horizon, or stay beside the light? 🕯️
